The lay's small edge over the back is not evidence of a lean: £10 lay liability at a long price is a smaller stake than a £10 back, so its pounds at risk per tick are smaller.

## Weaknesses
- **Small sample.** 63 signal markets and 30 or fewer fills per side at 103%+. The fall below control is not established; the verdict is that nothing reaches the bar.
- **Fill rule is pessimistic.** A print through the level is demanded, so a real order at the front of the queue at a new best price would fill more often and in less adverse moments. No queue model exists on this box, so this is unmeasured.
- **Halves.** All 103%+ h1 signals fall in the first 19 hours of the tape, and each half is a few fills.
- **Missing markets.** The 123 with no T-115 book may be the thinnest.
- **First run killed.** It reached the harness's 20-minute limit with no output; re-run unchanged but for line-buffered output. Fetch alone took 31 min (about 1,100 ticks/s), so the database was slow.
