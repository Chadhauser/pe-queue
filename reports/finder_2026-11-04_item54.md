## Reading
The call is about time decay. With no goal, Under and the draw shorten and Over and the trailing sides drift, nearly every minute; the model recognises the shape of that. In the 12% of holds with a goal the loss is about six times the win. At zero ticks the market is roughly fair, +£0.058 and +£0.025, and that is smaller than one tick each way. So the 488 puzzle has a plain answer, and goals, known here only to the test, are the whole difference: a price-only model cannot see them coming.

Limits: entries repeat within games, so the zero-tick t of 3.82 is inflated. LTP only, so real costs are higher, as the real touch runs about 3 ticks from LTP on item 37's draws. The goal split uses af minutes, so a goal near the exit may be misfiled. Decile thresholds come from in-sample 2024 predictions, so the state choice flatters 2024. No re-run needed.
