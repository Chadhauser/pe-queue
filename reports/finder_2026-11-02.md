## Item 3 — Racing BSP grid v3, honest version of 302 (jobs/451_racing_grid_v3.sh)
**Data:** racing_bsp UK/IE, 243,259 runners, 26,089 race-markets (WIN 127k, PLACE 116k), 2021-12-31 → 2026-10-12. Halves split at the date median (2024-05-03). Cells at n ≥ 300 (≥150 per half): 9,576.
**Rules:** one tick adverse at BSP entry and at every in-play exit. A take-profit fills only if ipmin/ipmax traded through the target by one tick. Untriggered exits settle at the result. 2% commission, £10 risk. Unit asserts on every shape pass.
**Caveats:** the band and steam cells use BSP, which isn't known when a BSP bet is placed (it stands in for the price at the off). The pp cells (ppwap includes the final pre-off trades) are excluded from PASS. Take-profit fills are touches, not proven fills.
**Two findings about 302:** (1) its "LAY_STOP +25/50/100% on ipmax" is really a lay *take-profit*, because a drift is profit for the layer. Kept here as LAY_TP. (2) A real lay stop (on ipmin) is **unmodellable** from this table. Every winner trades ~1.01, so "ipmin ≤ stop" caps every winner's loss, giving fake t ≈ 50 (e.g. LAY_STOP100 WIN 12-20: n 20,803, +£0.24, t 51.8). A stop can't rest on the book, and the in-play delay plus price gaps make that fill fiction. It's excluded.
