## Reading
The item's closing test: 2024 does rank 2025 somewhat, so the Segunda lead is not plainly selection, but it stays unproven: Segunda's own 2025 line is t 1.24. Laying Under in leagues chosen in advance is positive in every cut and both directions, always under t 2, with no line near the bar. Since the lay side is about zero on all games (+0.026), any effect is at most about 3 points in a few leagues. An edge of that size needs far more games than these two seasons hold. It also has to survive the real touch, which this LTP test does not measure. Do not re-run until a third season is on the box.
