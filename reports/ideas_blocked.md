# Ideas blocked: the data is not recorded (builder session 3, 20 Nov 2026)

These angles were NOT faked. Each line is the angle, and the one thing that is missing. Everything else from the session 3 brief is in the
league table (see /root/pe-builder/IDEAS.md for the full list with ticks). Measured on the recorded days 31 Oct to 19 Nov 2026.

| Angle | What cannot be tested | The one thing missing |
|---|---|---|
| 2. Drift before the start | Prices 24 hours and 6 hours before the start | The stream recorder starts each football market only 0.5 to 3 hours before kick-off (median 1.5 h on 15 Nov) and British and Irish races about 12 minutes before the off. It needs to subscribe 24 hours before. |
| 1, 2, 7, 8. Before the start in other sports | Tennis, cricket, golf, basketball, ice hockey, darts, snooker, American football before the start (the famous name at the price before the start, drift, offering, weight of money) | Betfair prices for these sports are recorded in play only (ip_tape). Needs pre-start recording of these sports. 'At the start' is tested as 30 seconds into play instead. |
| 7. Offering in racing | The last 30 minutes before the off (10, 5 and 2 minutes are in the table) | Racing quotes start about 12 minutes before the off. Needs the recorder to start 30 minutes before. |
| 7, 15. Greyhounds | Everything on greyhounds | No greyhound market is in the recorded prices at all. Needs greyhound win markets on the stream recorder, with results. |
| 1. Racing names | The favourite ridden or trained by the biggest names on 10 to 19 Nov | racecards_today stops at 9 Nov (jockey and trainer per horse). Needs the racecard fetch every day again. Rows are in the table and use 31 Oct to 9 Nov. |
| 1. Club popularity over a longer past | A club's popularity from more than the few matches it played since 2 Nov | Popularity is the money matched on a club's earlier match-result markets in our recordings (2 Nov onwards; 31 Oct and 1 Nov have no football match-result market recorded), so early days know few clubs (on 4 Nov only one match had both sides known). The history files (2019 on) hold last traded prices only, no matched money. Needs matched money per market in the history files, or simply more weeks of recording; coverage grows every day. |
| 1. Televised matches | Matches actually shown on television | No TV listings. Kick-off slots (weekday evening, Saturday 12:00 and 17:00, Sunday afternoon) are tested instead. Needs a TV listings feed. |
| 3, 4, 5, 7, 15. Quick in-play trades outside football and racing | Overreaction after a wicket, an ice hockey goal or a basketball run; closing 30 seconds later in tennis, cricket, basketball, golf, darts, ice hockey | Their in-play prices are polled every 32 seconds (ip_tape, measured on 16 Nov), against several updates a second on the stream for football and British and Irish racing. A quote is fresh (15 seconds) only half the time, a 30-second exit mostly cannot fill (held, as the test's rules say), and a suspension cannot be told from a polling gap, so event rows in these sports show 0 orders. Needs these sports on the stream recorder. (86 tennis markets on 16 Nov were on the stream and do work.) |
| 4. Red cards | Overreaction after a red card, apart from after a goal | Events are found from market suspensions, which a goal and a red card both cause. Needs the event type for each suspension (goal_timeline has goals only, for some fixtures). |
| 4, 5. Tennis by set | A lost set or a break of serve exactly | No point, game or set score in the data. Price swings stand in for them (rows in the table). Needs a point-by-point score feed. |
| 5. Basketball by quarter | The favourite behind at the end of a quarter | No quarter or score data. A drift from the price one minute in stands in (rows in the table). Needs a score feed with the quarter. |
| 4, 14. Fallers and leader changes in racing | Exactly when a horse falls or the lead changes | No in-running position data. Sharp price moves within a minute stand in (rows in the table, prices at most 2 seconds old). Needs in-running positions. |
| 6, 9. Next goal | Lay the next-goal outcomes; next goal against match odds | The next-goal market is not recorded. Needs NEXT_GOAL markets on the recorder. |
| 9. Tennis set betting | Set betting (2-0, 2-1 ...) against match odds | Only set winner markets are recorded, not set betting. Needs the set betting market recorded. |
| 9. Win and place in racing | Win against place before the off | Place markets are recorded in play only (529 markets, ip_tape); British and Irish place markets are not on the stream. Needs PLACE markets on the stream before the off. |
| 10. Offer on the slower exchange | Rest an order on Smarkets or Matchbook at Betfair's price | Smarkets and Matchbook trades are not recorded, so a resting order there cannot be shown to fill. Needs their traded prices and money. |
| 10. Which exchange moves first | Follow a Betfair move by taking the old price on Smarkets or Matchbook before it catches up | Smarkets and Matchbook prices are polled about every 24 seconds (median on 15 Nov; Matchbook 90% within 25 s), so we cannot tell a slow exchange from an old recording; a test would invent profit from prices that had already moved. Needs their prices at least every second (a streaming recorder). The cross test (take both sides when they cross after commission) is in the table, with quotes at most 30 s old. |
| 11. Early and late season | Early-season against late-season | We have 20 days (31 Oct to 19 Nov). Needs recorded prices across a whole season. |
| 11. Tennis surface and round | Surface (clay, grass, hard) and round | Neither is in the market or tournament names. Needs a tournament-to-surface table and the round per match. Tennis level (ITF, Challenger, ATP, WTA) is in the table. |
| 11. Going for Irish and all-weather detail | Going for races without a racecard | Going comes from racecards_today only (see the racing names line). |
