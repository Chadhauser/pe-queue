## Defect found: a final print is not a result
The first complete run settled a runner as the winner if its final in-play print was <= 1.05, the method several earlier jobs use. Its Over/Under table was impossible (priced 3-5: implied 0.285, won 0.634), so I tested the rule against real scores on 2,500 markets of each type (jobs/499_line_move_result_check.sh):

| Market | Matched | Rule agrees when it fires | Fires when | Fires when |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| OU25 | 1,134 | 97.7% | Under won 86.6% | Over won 5.1% |
| MO | 967 | 98.5% | Other results 93.9% | Draw 74.9% |

High agreement hides it: when Over wins, its last print is rarely <= 1.05 (the true winner's median last print is 1.15), so "known" markets are nearly all Under wins. The likely reason is the market closing at the third goal; that was not tested. That run's hold-to-result figures are void and not shown. After settling on real scores, the gate holds:

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| MO | 11,239 | 0.347 | 0.348 |
| OU25 | 5,086 | 0.508 | 0.511 |

Earlier jobs that use final prints need the same check, as they may be wrong in either direction → item 67.
