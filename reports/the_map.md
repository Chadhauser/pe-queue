- Caution on late in-play cells: a history print 80 minutes after the start is a median 58 s old (a quarter over 2.4 min, a tenth over 5 min; sample: over/under 2.5, first week of March 2025). In a goal market every quiet minute lowers the chance of more goals, so part of any late in-play gap is time passing since the last trade, not the market being wrong. The real-price check uses only quotes at most 15 s old and is the test that counts.
