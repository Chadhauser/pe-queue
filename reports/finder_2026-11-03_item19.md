## Defects found, for Peter (older results may shift)
1. **Own goals:** af_events.is_home already credits the team that benefits. Flipping it gives 962 FT-score mismatches, not flipping 41. The flip is in 293, 297, 303, 304, 307, 308, 309, 315, 323, 452, 453, 455 and 470. Home/away score states are wrong after an own goal, about 8% of games; total-goal states are not. Those jobs were not re-run tonight: item 38.
2. **Goal-minute exits:** any job exiting at af goal +2 on this tape carries the look-ahead above, including 323, 315, 452, 453 and 455.
3. **LTP is not the touch:** one tick from LTP understates cost on thin prices (BTTS Yes at 10-30: median 17, p90 40 ticks). Results on bf_hist_price are upper bounds for prices above about 10.
4. **Duplicate rows:** BTTS, OU15 and OU35 have more than one row per event/minute/selection (MO and OU25 none); values differ by > 20% on 216 of 338,849 keys sampled. Harmless to an array, wrong for "next row". It flattered 473's part A (+0.724; rebuilt +0.565).
