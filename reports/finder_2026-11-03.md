## Comparison with t2_paper
t2_paper is rebuilt every night by /root/pe-factory/t2_nightly.py. Tonight it holds n 70, settle **−£0.01**/£10 (28 Oct–1 Nov), not the +£0.33 quoted in the queue. Its hedge column is NULL. t2_nightly has three problems: (a) markets with no inferable winner are **dropped** (`if winner is None: continue`), which is the settled-market bias; on this tape that is 18% of entries, and counting them as losses moves settle from +£0.26 to −£1.68; (b) a flat 2% "spread" instead of the real touch plus one tick; (c) p0 is an in-play price.

## Verdict
**Nothing passes.** Even under the optimistic "known result only" treatment, the best full line is +£0.39/£10 at t 0.46 (n 165), and the hedge+20 exit loses in every cut. Six days of tape gives n 201. At about 33 qualifying markets a day, n≥300 is about 3 more days away, but at the current effect size t≥3.5 would need thousands of entries. **Caveat:** every sport on ip_tape starts on 28 Oct, although the brief says "since Sept". If ip_tape is pruned to a rolling ~6 days, it never accumulates, and the recorder would have to keep history for this test to become possible. The unknown-result share (18%) also needs fixing, e.g. by joining a results source or recording to market close.
