## Finding
The item's premise is **inverted** on this data. back_sz is money *offered by layers*, so imbalance ≥0.7 is followed by the price **lengthening** (football 20% vs 7%, racing 47% vs 35%), and ≤0.3 (heavy lay_sz, i.e. backers' money waiting) by shortening. The direction is consistent in both halves and both sports. But the average move is below one tick (football ±0.2, racing ≤ +1.0 ticks over 180 s) while the round trip costs the spread plus two ticks. So no touch-to-touch trade can pay, including the mirror "lay on ≥0.7" (same arithmetic).

## Verdict
**Nothing passes.** Weight of money predicts direction, too weakly to beat the spread over 60–180 s. It might still work as a timing filter for entries made for other reasons (not tested here).
