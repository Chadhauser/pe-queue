## Limits

- **Coverage:** real results exist for 39% of football events and 18% of cricket markets, mostly the bigger leagues and matches, and 17% of the worldwide racing tape. The rest has no truth here, so the verdict covers only what was scored.
- **Gate changed after the result:** the racing gate first failed at 16.8% of all tape races. A venue check showed the tape is worldwide and racing_results is UK/IE only, so the gate was re-based to UK/IE races, where it passes at 100%. Foreign firing rates are unmeasured.
- **Window:** racing is 30 Oct-4 Nov only, as ip_tape rolls.
- **Dropped markets:** cricket 40 markets (winner not matched to a runner); job 493's P25/P50/P75 states left out, as they look ahead and could never pass.
- **Ladder:** football uses the real ladder at tick edges (2.0 fills 1.99; 464 used 1.98).
- **Multiple tests:** 724 lines were tried, so a lone t near 3.5 would still deserve doubt.
