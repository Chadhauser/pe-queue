**How "through" was read:** a resting lay at X sits among the offers backers take at X, and backers take the highest price first. A print above X is someone else's better offer and fills nothing. Only a print strictly below X proves X has gone, so an order fills only when the price shortens against it. TOUCH (a print at X, assuming first in the queue) is a diagnostic. A stale ltp does not count: volume must rise on the tick. The exit is the runner's last pre-off tick; the age of that book was not re-checked here.
