**How the test works.** Every price update on our stream before the off is replayed. Per market, the quoter rests a back and (under 5.0 only) a lay at the best price, or one tick inside when the gap is 3+. It moves an order that is beaten, back to the end of the queue. Queue model: we sit behind the money shown; only money traded at our price gets to us; a trade beyond our price fills us in full. Through-only: only the trade beyond our price. After the first fill the other quotes are cancelled. The close is either moved with the market or held at one tick of profit; stop 1/2/3 ticks; flat 60/30/10 s before; at most £50 at risk; 2% commission per market. 20–21 Nov come straight from the live tables, settled markets only (21 Nov: results known by about 17:00 UTC).
**Checked.** 31 hand-worked cases in s10/test_mm.py, ALL PASS: queue eaten or not, a shrinking queue, a trade through, the stops, the time limit, BET held, no lay at 5.0+, one position per market, the £50 cap, commission, and both closing styles. The racing round-trip and stop sizes match tick arithmetic. **Fixed during the work:** the recorder counts traded money on both sides of a trade (£14.94 off the queue = £29.88 traded), so it is halved. Without that, queue fills would have been twice too easy. **Generous to the quoter:** no reaction delay, and a stop gets the next tick for anything beyond the size shown. So the real figures are, if anything, worse.
